Tracknov Service Level Agreement (SLA)

Last Updated: May 18, 2026

Commitment Guarantee: Tracknov commits to maintaining a monthly system uptime availability of no less than 99.9%.

1. Scope of Availability

Uptime includes document ingestion, OCR parsing pipelines, multi-tenant database isolation availability, and authorized reviewer interface availability.

2. Data Custody and Backup Schedule

  • Continuous real-time replication of system events to secondary servers.
  • Full backups are generated automatically every 24 hours.
  • Backup copies are stored with secure encryption across geographically separated datacenters.

3. Tenant Security and Isolation Levels

Every account is locked inside strict tenant boundary layers. Database operations enforce strict Row-Level Security (RLS), ensuring no cross-organization data leakage or visibility exists.

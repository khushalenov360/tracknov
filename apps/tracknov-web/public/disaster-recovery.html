Tracknov Disaster Recovery (DR) & Failover Protocol

Last Updated: May 18, 2026

Enterprise Target Guarantees:
• Recovery Point Objective (RPO): < 1 Minute (Maximum potential data gap during failover)
• Recovery Time Objective (RTO): < 15 Minutes (Maximum downtime during major system transition)

1. Redundancy and Failover Strategy

Tracknov utilizes multi-region secondary servers. If the primary cloud zone becomes unresponsive, load balancers will automatically re-route user request traffic to active standby zones with zero code modification.

2. Transaction and State Reconciliation

Our Certification Consistency engine verifies transaction history on startup. In the highly unlikely event of server disruption, we execute an automated state replay check to ensure submittals, checklists, and reviewer decision matrices remain 100% consistent and error-free.

3. Incident Communication Plan

During a high-severity outage, operational statuses are posted immediately to our real-time system dashboard. Post-incident forensics and resolution writeups are delivered to all tenant administrators within 24 hours of incident recovery.

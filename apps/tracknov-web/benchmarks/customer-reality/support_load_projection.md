## Deflection Performance Analysis
By providing friendly explanations of complex technical checks directly inside the support center, the system prevents users from lodging technical support requests. 

Furthermore, the **advisory-only** AI safety boundary prevents the support assistant from promising compliance outcomes or certification ratings, avoiding disputes and reducing manual operational overhead for support agents.
